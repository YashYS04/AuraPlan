The Engineering Principle

The AI provides judgment. Deterministic code does the arithmetic.

💰

Hamilton Budget Balancing

LLMs cannot sum decimals reliably. AuraPlan uses largest-remainder integer distribution so every cent sums to 100% of your budget.

📅

Dynamic Calendar Mapping

Relative milestone offsets are deterministically transformed into ISO dates. Any task already overdue is pulled to today and flagged.

📆

1-Click Calendar Sync (.ics)

Download standard RFC 5545 iCalendar files to sync your milestones instantly into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook.
